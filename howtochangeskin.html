How to change your skin on VoidSurvival.


Hello! cherrypepsi561 here (the owner of the server)... Here is how to change your skin on my server

We use a plugin called SkinsRestorer. It lets you import a skin to their API and load the skin..

First to upload a skin you have to load the webpage https://skinsrestorer.net/upload

Once your their you can upload the image (most likely a png file) into the webpage and hit generate

Once you hit generate it will give you a minecraft command. Use that command in chat in our minecraft server to wear the skin.
